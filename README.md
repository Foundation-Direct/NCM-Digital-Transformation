
# NCM Digital Transformation Technical Tracking Standards (Front-End Tools)

##Overview

Based on our own internal data, over 2/3s of the products available to dealerships do not pass standardized data, and less than 1/10 products pass accurate events that meet the ASC Specification. The goal of the NCM Digital Transformation is to provide an easy and light-weight implementation for products to be accurately measured without forcing every new product to also have to be experts in GA4, Tealium, Adobe, Matomo, or any other of the hundreds of website analytic tools.

By following this standard, we can insure that products are accurately represented to the thousands of NCM dealers in their 20 groups, and beyond.

##How to Utilize This Repository

Documentation is broken up into general sections, useful for more high level understandings as to the goal and generally what is being asked, as well as specific sections, that break out exactly what needs to be done for various products based on the type of service they offer, as well as the way they are implemented into the dealer's digital ecosystem.
